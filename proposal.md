## Executive Summary

## 1. Problem Definition & Context

It is impossible to ignore how AI is quickly taking over workplace tasks and redefing jobs in the job market. Improvements in AI give firms the option to automate repetitive and non qualitative work that can be done more effeciently, in lesser time, and cost less than paying a real person. AI has the potential to imrpove productivity but risks opening risks to inequality and labour displacement. AI is expected to effect jobs unevenly because of its robotic nature. This means it may negatively effect clerical type work while positively impacting high skilled work. This would raise conerns about the pay gap or job vacancy disparity between these 2 roles causing a large pay gap. 

Although AI is not expected to eliminate jobs in the near future, it is expected to reshape the tasks for different jobs in the industrial sector. The government now has an important job of creating policies that will allow the benefits of new technologies to be shared evenly and not just to one beneficial group.
